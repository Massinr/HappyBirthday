A letter, written with love

My dearest Alae

T

here are certain people who arrive in this world carrying something that cannot be explained, a particular grace, warmth that fills a room before they have spoken a single word. You are one of those rare people. And on this day, the day you came into existence and changed everything simply by being born, I find myself at a loss for words that feel worthy of you.

To know you is to understand what it means to be truly seen. You have a way of making the people around you feel that they matter, not through grand gestures, but through the quiet way you give your attention, your laughter, your care. That is not a small thing. That is everything.

I have watched you carry beauty effortlessly — not just in the way you look, but in the way you think, in the way you move through the world with an elegance that cannot be taught. You are, in the truest and most complete sense of the word, extraordinary.

So on your birthday, more than anything, I want you to feel how loved you are. Not just today, but always. Not just by me, but by life itself, which seems to have conspired to put someone as remarkable as you in it.

Happy birthday, Alae. May this year give back to you even a fraction of what you give to everyone around you.

Yours, always
From my heart to yours

What I wish for you this year

I That every morning this year greets you with something that reminds you life is beautiful.
II That you are loved in ways that feel safe, certain, and completely without condition.
III That your dreams, the ones you speak aloud and the ones you keep quietly to yourself, find their way to you.
IV That you are always surrounded by people who understand exactly how rare and wonderful you are.
V That you allow yourself, even for today, to feel truly celebrated because you have more than earned it.
With love, on your birthday
Happy Birthday

There is only one you, and the world is richer for it.

I love you to no end.
